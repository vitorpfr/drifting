Privacy Policy

Drifting — Last updated April 7, 2026

Drifting does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it stays.

Data stored on your device

The following information is saved locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Saved stations (favorites)
  • Listening preferences and taste profile
  • App settings (haptics, battery saving, sleep timer, etc.)
  • Drifting Plus purchase status

This data is not backed up to any server. If you delete the app, it is gone.

Radio station catalog

Drifting fetches a list of radio stations from Radio Browser, a public community-maintained directory. This is a standard network request — no account, no personal data, no tracking identifiers are sent. Your IP address is visible to Radio Browser's servers as with any website you visit.

In-app purchases

Drifting Plus is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. Drifting does not receive or store any payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase transaction.

Analytics and tracking

Drifting contains no analytics, no crash reporting services, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Children

Drifting does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
